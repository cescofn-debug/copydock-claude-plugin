# Copy Dock for Claude

Write and check product copy in your team's house style, right where you work.

**Your skill tells Claude the rules. Copy Dock proves every line follows them.**

## What you get

- **The Copy Dock skill.** Claude knows when copy is being written (button labels, error messages, empty states, onboarding, emails, string files, Figma text) and follows a write, check, fix loop every time. It includes guides for the most common kinds of copy.
- **The Copy Dock connector.** Claude reads your team's live voice rules, word list, banned words, approved examples and product facts from [Copy Dock](https://copydock.design), checks every line with plain code (length, banned words, word list, reading level, spelling, placeholders, dropped variables, unbacked claims), reuses wording your team already approved, and sends finished copy to your review queue.
- **`/copy-check`.** Checks the user-facing text in a file, a folder or your current changes, and lists exactly what to fix.

## Works before you sign up

Without a Copy Dock account, the skill uses a built-in starter style: plain words, clear errors, consistent buttons, no hype. Connect Copy Dock to use your team's own rules and the code-based checks.

## Setup

1. Install this plugin.
2. The first time Claude uses Copy Dock, sign in at copydock.design, pick your workspace and choose Allow. There's no key to copy.

Prefer to add the connector on its own? Its address is `https://copydock.design/api/mcp`.

## What Claude can and can't do in Copy Dock

Claude can read your house style, draft copy (kept in your history), check copy, search approved copy and add drafts to your review queue. It can't approve, edit or delete existing copy or change your house style. Everything it saves still needs a person's approval.

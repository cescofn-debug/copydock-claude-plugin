# Marketing headlines, landing pages and feature copy

Marketing copy earns attention with a clear, true promise, then backs it up.

## Headlines

- **Say the outcome for a specific reader**, not the category: "Every line of copy, checked before it ships", not "The AI copy platform".
- One idea. If it needs an "and", it's probably two headlines.
- Concrete beats clever. Wordplay that needs a second read loses most readers (and doesn't translate).

## Subheads and body

- The subhead says how the headline is true, in one or two sentences.
- Lead with the reader's problem or goal, then the product's answer.
- Feature copy = what it does + why it matters: "Checks every string in a pull request, so off-brand copy never reaches production."
- Short paragraphs, scannable lists, one call to action per section.

## Claims and proof

- **Only claim what you can back up.** Numbers, rankings ("#1", "the only", "fastest"), customer counts, prices, limits and comparisons need a source. With Copy Dock connected, leave `copy_kind` unset when checking so claims are tested against the product facts.
- If you don't have the fact, write a placeholder like [NUMBER OF CUSTOMERS] and flag it.
- Prefer specific proof over adjectives: a customer quote, a real metric, a screenshot.
- No hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "best-in-class", "unleash", "supercharge".

## Calls to action

- Name the next step and what they get: "Start free", "Book a 20-minute demo", "See it on your copy".
- Repeat the main call to action at the end of the page.

## Examples

| Don't | Do |
| --- | --- |
| The revolutionary AI-powered copy platform | Every line in your brand's voice, checked before it ships |
| Trusted by thousands of teams! | Trusted by [NUMBER] teams (confirm the number before shipping) |
| Supercharge your workflow | Write, check and approve copy where you already work |
| Get started | Connect your first product |

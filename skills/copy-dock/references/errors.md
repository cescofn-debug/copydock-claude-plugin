# Errors, warnings and failures

An error message is help at the worst moment. It has one job: get the person moving again.

## Structure

1. **What happened**, in their terms. "We couldn't save your changes."
2. **Why**, if it helps them act and you actually know. "The connection dropped."
3. **What to do next**, as a clear action. "Check your connection and try again."

Two short sentences usually cover it. Put the action in a button when there is one ("Try again", "Update card").

## Rules

- Never blame: "The card was declined", not "You entered an invalid card".
- Never vague: no "Something went wrong", "An error occurred" or "Oops!" on its own. If the cause is truly unknown, still give a next step: "That didn't go through. Try again, or contact support if it keeps happening."
- No codes on their own. A code can follow the message for support: "(Error 402)".
- No exclamation marks, no jokes, no "please".
- Keep their work safe and say so: "Your draft is saved."
- Field errors sit by the field and say how to fix it: "Enter a date after today", not "Invalid date".
- Warnings say what will happen and how to avoid it: "Your trial ends in 3 days. Add a card to keep your projects."

## Examples

| Don't | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Oops! Something went wrong!! | We couldn't load your projects. Refresh the page to try again. |
| Invalid input. | Use at least 8 characters for your password. |
| Payment failed. Error 402. | Your card was declined. Try another card or contact your bank. (Error 402) |
| Upload error. | 2 of 5 files didn't upload because they're over 25 MB. Make them smaller and try again. |
| You don't have permission. | Only admins can change billing. Ask an admin in your workspace. |

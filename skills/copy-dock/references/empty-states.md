# Empty states

An empty screen is the first time someone meets a feature, or a moment when there's nothing to show. Turn it into a next step.

## Know which empty it is

1. **First use:** nothing created yet. Explain the value in a line and give the first action.
2. **No results:** a search or filter found nothing. Say so and how to widen it.
3. **All done:** the person cleared everything (inbox zero, no tasks). Acknowledge it briefly; this is a good moment, a little warmth is fine.
4. **Not available:** no permission, or a plan limit. Say why and who can change it.

## Structure

- **Heading:** what this space is for, or what happened. "No projects yet".
- **One line:** why it matters or what to do. "Projects keep your copy, reviews and strings together."
- **One action:** the button that fills the space. "Create project". A second, quieter link is fine ("Import from Figma").

## Rules

- Don't just say "No data" or "Nothing here".
- Don't make the person feel behind ("You haven't done anything yet").
- For no results, repeat what they searched for and offer to clear filters.
- Keep it short. Empty states are glanced at, not read.

## Examples

| Situation | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| First use | **No projects yet** · Projects keep your copy, reviews and strings together. · [Create project] |
| No results | **No strings match "checkout"** · Try another word or clear the status filter. · [Clear filters] |
| All done | **You're all caught up** · New reviews and mentions will show up here. |
| No access | **Only admins can see billing** · Ask an admin in your workspace if you need an invoice. |
